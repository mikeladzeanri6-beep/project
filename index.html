FAQ

ხშირად დასმული კითხვებიFrequently asked questions

პასუხები Circadia-ს სათვალის, აპლიკაციისა და ყოველდღიური გამოყენების შესახებ.Answers about the Circadia glasses, the app and everyday use.

Hardware & Design

Weight and Comfort

Given that the frames house ambient light sensors, an AI processing unit, and a red LED strip, how much do the glasses weigh compared to traditional everyday eyewear?

Prescription Lenses

Are the smart lenses available with prescription (Rx) options for users who already wear corrective glasses, or are they currently only non-prescription?

Battery Life & Charging

How long does the battery last on a single charge while running the sensors and LED strip, and how are the glasses charged (e.g., USB-C, magnetic pogo pins, wireless case)?

Durability

Are the glasses water-resistant or sweat-resistant for users who might wear them during light exercise or in rainy weather?

LED Visibility

Is the red LED strip visible to other people looking at the wearer, and is the light distracting to the user's direct line of sight?

Technology & App Integration

Lens Technology

By what specific physical mechanism does the lens "manage" or adjust light levels during the day? Do they use electrochromic technology to actively darken and lighten?

AI Data Collection

The site mentions the AI learns your routine to create a personal circadian profile. Does it rely solely on the ambient light sensors, or does it also track movement, heart rate, or temperature?

App Ecosystem

Does the Circadia mobile app integrate with third-party health trackers (like Apple Health, Google Fit, or Oura) to cross-reference sleep data and improve the AI's accuracy?

Manual Overrides

Can the intensity of the evening red LED light be manually adjusted via the mobile app, or is it strictly dictated by the AI?

Jet Lag Mode

For the "future mode" Jet Lag feature, how does the system know your schedule? Do you input your flight details into the app so it can calculate the gradual light transition in advance?

Usability & Daily Routine

Wear Time

Do the glasses need to be worn continuously all day for the AI to build an accurate profile, or only during specific morning and evening windows?

Outdoor Use

Do the smart lenses offer UV400 protection, and do they transition dark enough to replace standard sunglasses when worn outdoors?

Screen Time

Do the lenses inherently block blue light from digital screens during the day, or is that managed entirely by the evening LED feature?

Standby Mode

Can the smart features be toggled off completely if the user just wants to wear them as regular glasses without any light adjustment?

Health & Efficacy

Clinical Backing

Are there specific clinical trials or scientific studies backing the efficacy of applying localized red LED light from a frame to improve sleep onset?

Long-term Safety

Is it entirely safe for eye health to have an artificial red LED light shining near the eyes every evening over a prolonged period?

Measurement of Success

Since the app displays "sleep progress," how exactly do the glasses know when you actually fall asleep and the quality of your sleep stages?
